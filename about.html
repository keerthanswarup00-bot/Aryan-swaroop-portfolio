It started with a broken laptop I took apart at 14.
Now I build brands for a living and code them into existence on
weekends, still not sure which one's the hobby.

In short

I'm from Bengaluru and still live here. The first thing I ever designed was a birthday banner for my mom in PowerPoint — I thought I was a graphic designer after that. I train PPL most days of the week, usually with friends.

Why brand design?

A client at my first job asked me to make a logo that "looked expensive but not try-hard." I spent a week on it, iterated 40 times, and delivered something they actually cried over. That was the turn — I realised the problem was never the logo, it was making someone feel something specific every time they saw it. That became the obsession.

Life outside work

Outside of client work I train PPL, usually with a couple of friends who keep me honest. I'm building toward a YouTube presence — same instinct that goes into a brand system, pointed at my own. Family dinner is non-negotiable, whatever the week looks like.

So what now?

Currently open to Creative Lead and Brand Designer roles — looking for a team where the work spans more than one industry.
