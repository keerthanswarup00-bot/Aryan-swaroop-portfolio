Case Study 01

Paavani Properties
End-to-End Brand System

Paavani needed a consistent brand system that could scale across more than 30 residential launches without each project feeling disconnected from the last. At the same time, every campaign had to communicate its own identity while strengthening the trust and recognition of the parent brand.

I built a unified creative system that brought consistency across branding, digital marketing, print, and video. The result was a scalable visual identity that not only maintained brand coherence but also helped turn every campaign into a stronger lead-generation opportunity.

Role

Creative Lead

Scope

Identity, print, 3D, film, campaign

Process

Brand system → collateral → 3D visualization → performance campaign

Result

302 leads in 66 days at ₹82 per lead
