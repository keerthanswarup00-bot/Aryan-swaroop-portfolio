Brahmi Coffee Roasters

Brand identity and packaging for a South Indian filter coffee brand — built to feel premium without losing what makes filter coffee feel like home.

ClientBrahmi Coffee Roasters
RoleBrand Identity & Packaging
ScopeLogo, color system, typography, packaging, marketing collateral
ToolsIllustrator, Photoshop, InDesign

Brief

Build a modern coffee brand that could stand next to international specialty brands without losing the warmth of South Indian filter coffee culture — the ritual, not just the product.

The Problem

Most specialty coffee brands lean on dark industrial minimalism or Western café aesthetics. It reads as premium, but it strips out the parts that actually make filter coffee what it is — brass filters passed down through a family, the smell before sunrise, a conversation that starts over the first cup. Nobody was designing for how the coffee actually feels, only for how it looks on a shelf.

Process

Skipped the expected coffee-brown palette for a deep teal, paired with brass-toned accents pulled directly from the traditional filter itself — the color set the brand apart on shelf while the brass kept it rooted in the ritual it came from. The harder call was avoiding every cliché in the coffee-branding playbook: no beans, no steam, no café illustrations. Distinction had to come from color, type, and composition alone, not from the obvious imagery every competitor already uses.

Result

A packaging system built to stand apart on a shelf crowded with international coffee brands, while staying recognizably rooted in South Indian coffee culture — designed to extend across future products without a redesign.
