Featured Case Study

Sastry's by Brahmi

Brahmi needed a packaging system that could earn its place on a premium shelf without losing its authentic South Indian filter coffee roots. Most regional coffee brands felt either outdated in their presentation or stripped of the heritage that made them distinctive.

I built the identity from the ground up to balance both worlds. Every design decision was made to celebrate tradition while presenting the brand with a refined, contemporary aesthetic that feels premium, memorable, and unmistakably rooted in its origin.

Role

Brand & Packaging Designer

Scope

Identity, packaging, typography

Process

Positioning research → logo & type system → packaging design → scalable SKU rollout

Result

Brand system completed end-to-end, full SKU line ready for retail.

About
